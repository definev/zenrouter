# ZenRouter benchmark baseline — 2026-08-17

Comparison of the current workspace against `zenrouter 2.2.0`, whose published
dependency resolves to `zenrouter_core 2.1.0`.

Environment: Dart 3.13.0 AOT, macOS 26.5.2 arm64, 10 logical processors. The
workspace was dirty at commit `b1c0479c27b8da8bede8cb81b4fe81a5c6c0d427`.

Configuration: 5 alternating trials, 12 samples per trial, 100,000 base
iterations per sample, and 10,000 base warm-up iterations. Lower is better.

| Benchmark | Old median ns/op | Current median ns/op | Delta | Speedup |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `path_push_pop` | 552.6 | 545.5 | −1.3% | 1.013x |
| `path_push_or_move_to_top` | 381.7 | 280.5 | −26.5% | 1.361x |
| `path_navigate_depth_32` | 3,870.3 | 4,541.5 | +17.3% | 0.852x |
| `coordinator_replace_flat` | 390.0 | 1,268.3 | +225.2% | 0.308x |
| `coordinator_navigate_flat` | 326.9 | 1,204.7 | +268.5% | 0.271x |
| `coordinator_push_fallback` | 396.3 | 1,432.0 | +261.4% | 0.277x |
| `coordinator_replace_nested` | 658.0 | 1,812.5 | +175.4% | 0.363x |
| `coordinator_create_dispose` | 1,372.1 | 1,532.0 | +11.7% | 0.896x |

AOT executable: old 5,896,880 bytes (5.62 MiB), current 5,946,128 bytes
(5.67 MiB), +0.84%.

Coordinator operations are 2.7–3.7× slower because each call now runs inside
`runNavigationTransaction`, which drains a microtask at the commit boundary.
That is extra completion/error-propagation work, not a like-for-like increase
in synchronous CPU. Path-level `pushOrMoveToTop` improved; `navigate` through
a 32-route stack is slower because `pop` now completes the route result and
calls `onDidPop` instead of deferring that to a Flutter page callback.

minimum, maximum, environment metadata, and all 60 raw samples per benchmark.
