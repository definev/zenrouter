# ZenRouter benchmark baseline — 2026-08-13

Comparison of the current workspace against `zenrouter 2.2.0`, whose published
dependency resolves to `zenrouter_core 2.1.0`.

Environment: Dart 3.13.0 AOT, macOS 26.5.2 arm64, 10 logical processors. The
workspace was dirty at commit `e1c2bfac31cb4a0d3442bc286271261f19007e9b`.

Configuration: 5 alternating trials, 12 samples per trial, 100,000 base
iterations per sample, and 10,000 base warm-up iterations. Lower is better.

| Benchmark | Old median ns/op | Current median ns/op | Delta | Speedup |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `path_push_pop` | 551.0 | 537.4 | −2.5% | 1.025x |
| `path_push_or_move_to_top` | 372.8 | 373.0 | +0.1% | 0.999x |
| `path_navigate_depth_32` | 3,844.3 | 3,656.6 | −4.9% | 1.051x |
| `coordinator_replace_flat` | 384.4 | 385.0 | +0.2% | 0.998x |
| `coordinator_navigate_flat` | 318.6 | 319.4 | +0.3% | 0.997x |
| `coordinator_push_fallback` | 389.2 | 430.3 | +10.6% | 0.905x |
| `coordinator_replace_nested` | 650.7 | 753.3 | +15.8% | 0.864x |
| `coordinator_create_dispose` | 1,349.8 | 1,351.0 | +0.1% | 0.999x |

AOT executable size was identical: 5,896,880 bytes for both implementations.

The two slower cases cross asynchronous activation boundaries. The current
implementation awaits `activateRoute` and parent-layout activation, whereas
2.2.0 discarded those futures in these branches. Their deltas therefore
include stronger completion and error-propagation semantics and are not a
like-for-like increase in synchronous CPU work.
