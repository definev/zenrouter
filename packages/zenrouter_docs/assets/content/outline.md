# ZenRouter documentation outline

This file controls chapter ordering and navigation labels. Use one chapter per
line in the format `- NN | Title | Outcome`. Chapter prose remains in the
matching `chapter-NN.md` file and is loaded only when a reader opens it.

## I · Start with the graph

- 01 | Start here | See the problem ZenRouter is designed to make boring.
- 02 | Choose a navigation model | Pick the smallest routing surface that fits the job.
- 03 | A route graph in five minutes | Connect routes, paths, layouts, manifests, and bindings.

## II · Build Compass

- 04 | Install ZenRouter 3 | Set up the 3.0 beta and choose handwritten or generated routes.
- 05 | Build your first graph | Create a typed home route and parameterized article route.
- 06 | Navigate without string drift | Keep forward matching and reverse URLs aligned.

## III · Shape the app

- 07 | Paths and layouts | Give each shell ownership of the stack it renders.
- 08 | Tabs and retained branches | Choose indexed destinations or independent branch histories.
- 09 | URLs, parameters, and 404s | Make a link mean the same thing throughout the app.
- 10 | Guards and redirects | Separate leaving a route from resolving an arrival.
- 11 | Restoration and recovery | Restore a navigation context, not merely one screen.

## IV · Grow with confidence

- 12 | File-based routing | Generate the same route graph from a directory.
- 13 | Scale the graph | Compose feature fragments and nested coordinators.
- 14 | See what the router sees | Read topology and observed flow in DevTools.
- 15 | Test navigation contracts | Test round trips and commits at their seams.

## V · Field guides

- 16 | Authentication flow | Protect a route without losing the incoming link.
- 17 | Bottom navigation | Match tab behavior to the product retention promise.
- 18 | State management | Keep routes small and domain state in its own store.
- 19 | Migrate to 3.0 | Adopt the manifest seam without rewriting every screen.
- 20 | Coming from another router | Translate concepts instead of syntax.
